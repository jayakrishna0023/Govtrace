## 1. Commercial Strategy: The L1 Winning Proposition

In government procurement, technical excellence qualifies a bid, but **commercial competitiveness (L1 - Lowest Responsive Compliant Bidder)** secures the tender award.

Traditional enterprise IT bids for government supply chain tracking routinely exceed **₹3.0 Crores to ₹10.0 Crores** due to:
- Proprietary ERP database licenses (Oracle/SAP/IBM) costing ₹60 Lakhs+ annually.
- Per-user seat taxes and per-transaction / per-QR scan metering.
- Bloated implementation teams billing multi-year customization hours.

### The GovTrace Cost Advantage:
1. **Zero Proprietary License Fee:** GovTrace is built on enterprise open-source primitives (PostgreSQL, Linux, Python/FastAPI, Hyperledger Fabric). The government pays strictly for software deployment and engineering support—**never for recurring third-party licenses**.
2. **Sovereign In-State Cloud Efficiency:** Runs on existing **State Data Centre (SDC)** or **NIC MeghRaj**, requiring minimal compute (saving ₹20 Lakhs/year in cloud bills).
3. **8 Ready-to-Use Sectors:** Pre-configured workflows for Food, Dairy, Pharma, Cement, Textiles, Spices, Mining, and Crafts eliminate expensive custom coding cycles.
4. **Transparent, Predictable Fixed Cost:** Zero surprise charges, zero per-citizen scan fees, and unlimited department user accounts.

## 6. Commercial Terms & Conditions

1. **Firm and Fixed Price:** The quoted rates are firm and fixed for the entire duration of the contract (3 years). No price escalation on any account shall be permissible.
2. **Statutory Taxes:** Goods and Services Tax (GST) will be charged at prevailing government rates (presently 18%). Any statutory increase or decrease in taxes shall be passed on to the government.
3. **Hardware / Cloud Infrastructure:** Compute resources (virtual machines on NIC MeghRaj / State Data Centre) and SMS gateway credits will be provided by the procuring department. GovTrace engineering will optimize sizing to keep resource consumption minimal.
4. **Source Code & Sovereign Ownership:** Upon completion of milestone payments, the procuring government agency holds perpetual rights to deploy, modify, and maintain the software for any state department without additional license fees.
5. **No Hidden Costs:** The quote includes all documentation, system integration, bug remediation, security patches, and remote/onsite technical support.
